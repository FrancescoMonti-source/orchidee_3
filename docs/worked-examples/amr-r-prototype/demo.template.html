Can AMR for R work with Rouen’s data?

This trial sends real local organism and laboratory result labels to AMR, keeps its mapping suggestions visible, and calculates a small resistance table. Use the buttons to inspect mappings and follow a resistant-isolate count through to its tested denominator.

Exploratory, undeduplicated data. All raw sample types are included; diagnostic selection and hospital perimeter selection have not been applied. These figures are not ORCHIDEE surveillance indicators. Identical rows for one isolate–molecule result are collapsed; separate isolates from the same patient remain separate.

R means resistant; S means susceptible at standard exposure; I means susceptible with increased exposure under the EUCAST convention. The French source category SFP has the latter meaning. ZIT is a technical uncertainty flag; this trial follows ORCHIDEE’s explicit decision to map it to I as well.
