# 02 - Establishments included and excluded

Status: **not started**.

## What SPARES says

Included: all establishments with complete and/or weekly hospitalisation, and
EHPAD holding a pharmacie a usage interieur.

Excluded: MECSS, hospitalisation a domicile, ambulatory dialysis.

## Open
