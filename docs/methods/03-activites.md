# 03 - Activities included and excluded

Status: **not started**.

## What SPARES says

Included: complete and weekly hospitalisation in medicine (with intensive care,
continuous monitoring, lits-porte and very short stay units), surgery,
resuscitation, paediatrics, gynaecology-obstetrics, SSR/SMR, long-term care and
adult psychiatry.

Excluded: retrocession, venues, seances, journees de prise en charge,
consultations, passages aux urgences, UCSA.

> La dispensation d'antibiotiques et la realisation de prelevements dans les
> unites de soins intensifs et unites de surveillance continue specialisees sont
> affectees a la discipline correspondante.
