# 09 - Analysis and publication

Status: **not started**.

## What SPARES says

Analysis is carried out by the national mission, beginning with a coherence and
plausibility check of the whole database. Results feed local reports, regional
and national indicators, and European reporting (EARS-Net, GLASS).

## Open

- **The divergence account.** ORCHIDEE must explain, indicator by indicator, why
  its number differs from the ConsoRes number, attributing the gap to named
  decisions from this document. Settled in principle; the form is not designed.
- What plausibility checks does ORCHIDEE run on its own output, and does a
  failure block publication or annotate it?
- Delivery format and cadence to SPF, and whether a revised figure replaces or
  supplements one already sent (see `01-periode-surveillance.md`).
- The local report for the hospital: a consumer of the indicator table, never a
  second implementation.
